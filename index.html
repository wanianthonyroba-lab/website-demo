Our Story

Ravenz General Trading Company Ltd was established by a team of highly qualiﬁed vibrant individuals in 2023, since then the company is performing steadily in the sector of general supply and service provision to both the local and regional market.
Standing on it’s values of integrity, quality assurance, transparency and innovation, Ravenz General Trading Ltd was established to solve problems faced by private institutions, NGOs and Government institutions of delayed, low quality and unreliable delivery of products and services.

Our Mission

To provide a wide range of high-quality products and services to meet the diverse needs of customers. This includes sourcing and distributing a variety of goods, building strong relationships with suppliers and customers, and ensuring efﬁcient and reliable delivery of products. The company aims to constantly innovate and adapt to market trends, while maintaining a commitment to ethical business practices and sustainability. Ultimately, the mission is to be a trusted and valuable partner for both customers and suppliers, contributing to the success and growth of all stakeholders involved.

Our Vision

To be a leading provider of diverse, high-quality products and services, meeting the needs of customers across various industries and markets.
To build strong and lasting relationships with customers, suppliers, and partners, based on trust, reliability, and mutual beneﬁt.
To continuously innovate and adapt to changing market dynamics, staying ahead of industry trends and providing innovative solutions to customers.
